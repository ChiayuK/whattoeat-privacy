# whattoeat-privacy
WhatToEat Privacy Policy
Privacy Policy for WhatToEat

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how WhatToEat ("we", "us", or "our") 
collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our 
mobile application WhatToEat (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

Location Information
We collect your device's location information when you use the 
distance filtering feature. This information is used only within 
the App to calculate distances to restaurants. We do not store or 
transmit your location to our servers.

Restaurant Data
All restaurant information you add (names, addresses, photos, 
ratings, and notes) is stored locally on your device using 
UserDefaults. This data is not transmitted to any server.

Advertising
We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in the App. 
AdMob may collect and use data including:
- Device identifiers (IDFA)
- IP address
- General location
- App usage data

This data is used to provide personalized advertisements. 
You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings:
Settings → Privacy → Tracking → Disable for WhatToEat

For more information about how Google uses this data, please visit:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:
- Display nearby restaurant distances (location data)
- Provide relevant advertisements (AdMob data)
- Improve App functionality

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with 
third parties except:
- Google AdMob (advertising partner)
- As required by law

4. Data Retention

Restaurant data is stored locally on your device and can be 
deleted by uninstalling the App. We do not retain any personal 
data on our servers.

5. Children's Privacy

WhatToEat does not knowingly collect personal information from 
children under 13. If you believe we have collected information 
from a child under 13, please contact us.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will 
notify you of any changes by updating the date at the top of 
this policy.

7. Contact Us
